## 2. IN-DEPTH TECHNICAL EVALUATION

### 2.1 Why Paper / LDPE is REJECTED for Long Shelf-Life Agarbatti
In rural and cottage agarbatti manufacturing, Kraft paper poly-coated with LDPE ($70\text{ gsm} / 20\ \mu\text{m}$) is widely used due to historical familiarity and perceived "organic" appearance. However, rigorous packaging science invalidates this choice for scented sticks:
1. **Terpene Scalping and Dissolution:** LDPE is non-polar and semi-crystalline with high free volume. Fragrance carrier solvents (Diethyl Phthalate - DEP, Dipropylene Glycol - DPG) and volatile terpenes ($\alpha$-pinene, limonene, linalool) dissolve directly into the amorphous regions of the LDPE layer. Once penetrated, these oils soak into the fibrous Kraft paper backing, causing unsightly translucent grease stains and complete aroma loss.
2. **Moisture Permeability:** With a WVTR of $18.0\text{ g/m}^2/\text{day}$, moisture enters through the paper and poly layer in tropical monsoon conditions ($35^\circ\text{C}, 80\%\text{ RH}$), raising product moisture above the critical mold threshold ($14\%$) in under 7 days!

### 2.2 Why Metallized PET / LDPE is the WINNING SELECTION
* **The Aluminum Nano-Barrier:** Metallized PET features a vacuum-deposited nano-scale aluminum coating ($0.03 - 0.05\ \mu\text{m}$, Optical Density $2.2 - 2.8$). This crystalline metallic layer reduces moisture diffusion by $96\%$ compared to unmetallized film and reduces oxygen ingress to $1.2\text{ cc/m}^2/\text{day}$.
* **Aroma Hermeticity:** PET's high glass transition temperature ($T_g \approx 78^\circ\text{C}$) and tight polar backbone prevent organic vapor absorption. The fragrance cannot scalp into the substrate.
* **Puncture Resistance:** Agarbatti sticks have sharp, hand-cut bamboo stick tips (diameter $1.2 - 1.5\text{ mm}$). Biaxially oriented PET substrate provides $48.0\text{ N}$ dart impact resistance and $28.0\text{ N/15mm}$ peel strength, preventing tip puncture during transit.
* **Cost Efficiency:** At $\text{INR } 9.20/\text{m}^2$, a standard $260\text{ mm} \times 45\text{ mm}$ pouch consumes only $0.0234\text{ m}^2$ of laminate, resulting in a raw pouch material cost of only **$\text{INR } 0.215$ (21.5 paise)**!

### 2.3 Sustainable / Compostable Alternative (Regulatory Contingency)
Where state or local municipal regulations strictly prohibit multi-layer non-recyclable metallized laminates, the recommended alternative is:
* **Cellulose / AlOx High-Barrier Bio-Film (NatureFlex) or Paper / Water-Based Barrier Dispersion Coating.**
* **Trade-off:** Tropical shelf life drops to $45 - 60\text{ days}$, and pouch material cost rises to $\text{INR } 0.52\text{ per pouch}$.
