SimScale Setup Checklist

1. CAD Preparation
Extract internal flow volume. Remove fillets < 2mm. Simplify tray mesh to porous media equivalent blocks (saves mesh elements).
2. Material Assignment
Air: Incompressible, Boussinesq approx for buoyancy. Solid: SS304 (k=16), CRCA (k=43), PUF (k=0.022). PCM modeled as solid with specific heat curve.
3. Mesh Setup
Hex-dominant parametric. Refinement regions: Louver wake zone (level 3), Heater vicinity (level 4), Boundary layers: 5 layers, expansion ratio 1.2.
4. Boundary Conditions
Inlet: Volumetric flow 100 m³/h. Outlet: Pressure outlet (0 Pa). Heater: Volumetric heat source (580W). Walls: Convection heat transfer coeff (h=5 W/m²K, T_ext=25°C).
5. Solver Settings & Post-Processing
k-omega SST turbulence model. Transient simulation: 0.1s timestep for louver sweep, pseudo-transient for thermal warmup. Setup probe points at all 10 tray centers.
