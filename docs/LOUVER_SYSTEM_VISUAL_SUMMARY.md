## **JUDGE SUMMARY (60 SECONDS)**

> **The Problem:** Agarbatti's economic value comes from fragrance (essential oils). But traditional sun-drying reaches 70°C, destroying 40% of these oils. Artisans get paid for lower-grade product (Rs 18/kg instead of Rs 32/kg).
>
> **The Innovation:** A servo-driven thermal louver that maintains a 40–45°C temperature ceiling. When solar air exceeds 45°C, the louver opens and mixes cool ambient air—preserving fragrance via airflow, not baking. Cost: 3,500 INR.
>
> **The Impact:** 
> - Fragrance retention: 55% → 93% (+38%)
> - Market grade: Defective → Premium
> - Artisan income: Rs 18/kg → Rs 32/kg (+77% price)
> - Monthly income gain: Rs 2,500/person
> - Deployment: 1,850 artisans across 5 states
> - ROI: 27.5× (subsidy multiplier)
>
> **Why It Wins:** Physics (passive solar draft) + intelligent control (servo louver) + artisan economics (income multiplication) + scalability (off-grid, cluster-deployable). No complex machinery. No grid dependency. Pure engineering for rural livelihood transformation.
